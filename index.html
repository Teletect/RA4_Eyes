Use

Point the camera at a neutral gray or white patch on the print. Use Manual to enter CMY viewing filters and Density, tap Sample once, or switch to Auto to follow the center patch continuously. Make only the displayed M/Y enlarger move, then run a confirmation strip.

The math uses ideal CC density filters. Actual RA-4 papers, color heads, lenses, lamps, and camera white balance vary, so treat the result as a fast starting pack.
