Use

Point the camera at a neutral gray or white patch on the print. Use Manual to enter CMY values, tap Sample once, or switch to Auto to follow the center patch continuously. Add the displayed correction to the enlarger pack, then run a confirmation strip.

The math uses ideal CC density filters. Actual RA-4 papers, color heads, lenses, lamps, and camera white balance vary, so treat the result as a fast starting pack.
